# 3-Channel Rollout Plan + Realistic Sizing

Honest numbers for running the Personal Finance / Make Money Online / Legal
A/B/C test. Read this **before** committing — running 3 channels is real work,
and the biggest risk is burning out before any of them monetize.

---

## ⏱️ How long one 10-min faceless video actually takes

| Step | Careful (quality) | Automated (speed) |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Script prose (fill 7 segment prompts in Claude + edit) | 45 min | 20 min |
| Voiceover (ElevenLabs v3) | 15 min | 10 min |
| Visuals (charts + stock B-roll, timed to cues) | 90 min | 45 min |
| Assembly + captions (NLE) | 90 min | 45 min |
| Thumbnail + SEO upload | 30 min | 20 min |
| **Total per video** | **~4.5 hrs** | **~2.3 hrs** |

The Channel Studio tool removes the *blank-page* time (script scaffold, prompts,
visual cue list, SEO, mid-roll marks all auto-generated). It does **not** remove
edit/render time — that's the real bottleneck.

## 📅 Cadence reality check

YouTube rewards **consistency**, not volume. Minimum viable = **1 video/week per
channel**.

| Plan | Videos/week | Your time/week (automated) | Sustainable solo? |
|------|-------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| 3 channels × 1/wk | 3 | ~7 hrs | ✅ Yes |
| 3 channels × 2/wk | 6 | ~14 hrs | ⚠️ Tight (part-time job) |
| 1 channel × 3/wk | 3 | ~7 hrs | ✅ Yes (faster growth, 1 niche) |

> **The honest tradeoff:** 3 channels at 1/wk each grows all three *slowly*.
> 1 channel at 3/wk grows *one* much faster to monetization. The A/B/C test is
> worth it ONLY if you can hold ~7 hrs/week for ~3 months without seeing money.

## 🗓️ The realistic timeline (don't expect money fast)

**Monetization bar (long-form):** 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch-hours in 12 months.

| Phase | Weeks | What happens | Revenue |
|-------|-------|--------------|---------|
| **0. Setup** | Week 0 | Create 3 channels, banners, about pages, generate first batch of briefs | $0 |
| **1. Seed** | Weeks 1–8 | Publish 1/wk per channel (24 videos total). Curator says KEEP_TESTING. | $0 |
| **2. First read** | Weeks 9–12 | Each channel has ~10 videos → curator gives real DOUBLE_DOWN/HOLD/CUT. | $0 (likely still pre-monetization) |
| **3. Concentrate** | Months 4–6 | CUT the loser, pour its time into the winner(s). One channel likely nears the monetization bar. | $0 → first $ |
| **4. Monetize** | Months 6–9 | Winner crosses 1k/4k, AdSense turns on. | first real RPM income |
| **5. Scale** | Month 9+ | Reinvest into the proven niche; consider outsourcing editing. | compounding |

> **Reality:** plan for **~6 months of unpaid runway.** Anyone promising faster
> is selling something. The upside: a finance channel past 100k subs can do
> $5k–50k/mo from AdSense alone — but that's the *destination*, not month 2.

## ✅ Recommended path for you

Given solo + ~7 hrs/week realistic:

1. **Weeks 1–8:** Run the A/B/C test at **1 video/week per channel** (the
   minimum that's fair to each niche). Use the tool to batch-generate briefs so
   blank-page time is zero.
2. **Week 9 (the decision point):** Run `curate`. Let the data — not your gut —
   pick the winner.
3. **Months 3–6:** **Cut to 1 channel** (the winner) at 3/week. Concentration
   beats spreading thin once you have signal.

This gets you the *insurance* of testing 3 niches AND the *focus* of betting on
one — without needing to sustain 3 channels forever.

## ⚠️ The one thing that kills this

Not picking the wrong niche — **inconsistency**. Three channels abandoned at
video 6 = zero. One channel at video 30 = a real shot. If 7 hrs/week isn't
realistic for you right now, **start with ONE channel (finance)** and add the
others only once your workflow is fast. The tool supports both — it's a
scheduling/energy decision, not a technical one.
