# Sample Short #01 — "10 Quiet Minutes" (ASMR coloring + paint-mix hook)

Funnel: **ASMR coloring channel** → **Self-Care Sunday** book on Etsy/KDP.
Format: vertical **9:16**, ~20–25s, faceless, loopable.
Goal: satisfying watch → "I want that book" → bio link.

---

## The hook (first 1.5s — this is 80% of the battle)
- **On-screen text:** `POV: 10 quiet minutes that are just for you 🤍`
- **Visual:** extreme close-up of a paintbrush dropping color into water / a
  marker starting to fill a line-art bathtub page. Motion + ASMR from frame 1.
- **Audio:** soft marker/brush ASMR + a calm lo-fi track (low volume).

bold clean outlines, thick lines, no shading, pure white background,
